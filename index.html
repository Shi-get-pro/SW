// About

Leading the Shift to AI-Powered Experiences

Enterprises don't need more AI pilots. They need leaders who can connect AI to real customer problems, align the organization behind it, and prove the value. That is the work I do.

As Associate Director of Business Technology & AI at Verizon Business, I lead the move from reactive service to AI-powered, self-serve journeys for business customers. I set the roadmap, align business, engineering and design around one view of the customer, and hold every initiative to a clear metric.

My foundation in mathematics, business analytics and Lean Six Sigma means decisions start with evidence. Two decades across Telecom, Internet and Automotive, including Bharti Airtel, mean I've seen customer experience at every scale, from a branch floor to a national carrier.

I'm most energized by work where AI, product and customer experience meet at enterprise scale, and by building the leaders who will carry it forward.

How I Lead
